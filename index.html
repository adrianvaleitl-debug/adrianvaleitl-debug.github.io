06 / HOW I WORK

Ideas, built properly.

A little more about the thinking behind the software, the details I care about and what I want each project to feel like.

Useful first.

I look for everyday problems where a small, well-made tool can save time, remove friction or make something easier to understand.

Designed to feel clear.

Simple navigation, readable layouts and sensible defaults matter just as much as the feature list.

Built to keep improving.

Projects are developed in versions, with fixes and improvements shaped by real use rather than adding complexity for its own sake.

Independent projects.

Room to experiment.
